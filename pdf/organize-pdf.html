How to Reorder and Delete PDF Pages

  1. Upload PDF: Select your file. Each page will render into an interactive visual card.
  2. Reorder Pages: Use the left (⬅️) and right (➡️) arrows to move pages to your desired order.
  3. Remove Pages: Click the trash icon (🗑️) to delete any unwanted or blank pages. Click it again anytime to undo.
  4. Export: Click "Save & Download PDF" to assemble your new streamlined PDF instantly.
