How to Delete Pages from a PDF

  1. Upload PDF: Select your file. High-resolution thumbnails are instantly generated in your browser.
  2. Mark Pages to Delete: Click directly on any page thumbnail to flag it for removal (marked with a red deletion tag). You can also type page numbers or ranges (e.g. 2, 4-7).
