How PDF Cropping Works

When you crop a document with RapidTables.online, our engine updates the document's internal CropBox and MediaBox coordinates via pdf-lib. This non-destructively trims page edges and white margins while preserving original vector resolution and selectable text.

Benefits of Trimming PDF Margins

  • E-Reader & Tablet Optimization: Removing giant white paper margins makes pages much larger and easier to read on Kindle and iPads.
  • Trim Printer Marks & Bleeds: Cleanly remove crop marks, color bars, or printer registration lines from pre-press PDFs.
  • 100% In-Browser Privacy: No document is ever sent over the internet or saved to external servers.
