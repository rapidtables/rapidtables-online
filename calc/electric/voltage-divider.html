Voltage Divider Formula

A voltage divider turns a larger voltage into a smaller one using two resistors connected in series:

Vout = Vin × [ R2 / (R1 + R2) ]
  • Vin: Input power supply voltage
  • R1: First resistor connected to Vin
  • R2: Second resistor connected to Ground (GND)
  • Vout: Output voltage tapped between R1 and R2
