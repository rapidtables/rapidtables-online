Lossless PNG vs. JPEG for PDFs

Unlike JPEG, which introduces compression artifacts around text borders and sharp edges, PNG provides 100% pixel-perfect lossless representation. This is essential for engineering blueprints, architectural plans, vector charts, and scientific diagrams.
