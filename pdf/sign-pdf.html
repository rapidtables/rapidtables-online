How to Sign a PDF Document Online

  1. Upload PDF: Select your agreement, invoice, or PDF document.
  2. Create Signature: Draw your signature using your mouse, trackpad, or touchscreen. Alternatively, switch to "Type Signature" for a cursive signature.
  3. Choose Placement: Pick whether to sign the last page, first page, or all pages, and choose the alignment.
  4. Download: Click "Sign Document & Download" to generate the signed PDF immediately.
