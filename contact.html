Frequently Asked Questions

Are all tools on RapidTables.online completely free?

Yes, 100%. All calculators, converters, PDF tools, and developer utilities are free to use with zero subscriptions, credit card requirements, or feature limits.

Do you store or save any of my data, files, or calculations on your servers?

No, never. We do not store, track, or collect any user data whatsoever. RapidTables.online is 100% client-side native: all calculations, conversions, notes, documents, and PDF files are processed directly inside your own computer or device's browser memory (RAM). Absolutely nothing is uploaded, sent, or saved to any external server.

Can I use generated barcodes and QR codes for commercial products?

Yes! All vector SVGs and PNG barcodes generated via our tools are completely royalty-free for commercial merchandise, product packaging, print advertisements, and digital publishing.

How do I report a mathematical bug or calculation discrepancy?

Select "Math / Formula Correction" in the form above, mention the tool URL, your input parameters, expected result, and observed result. Our engineers review and verify every report.
