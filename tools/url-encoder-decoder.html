URL Encoder & Decoder

Convert text and parameters into percent-encoded URLs or decode encoded URL strings back into readable text.

What is URL Encoding (Percent-Encoding)?

URL encoding converts characters into a format that can be safely transmitted over the Internet. URLs can only be sent over the Internet using the standard ASCII character-set.

  • Non-ASCII characters and reserved characters (e.g. spaces, &, =, ?, /) are replaced with a % followed by two hexadecimal digits.
  • For example, a space character becomes %20.
