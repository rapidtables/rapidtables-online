How to Calculate Fractions

A fraction represents part of a whole, written as Numerator / Denominator.

  • Adding & Subtracting: Convert fractions to a common denominator before adding or subtracting the numerators.
  • Multiplying: Multiply the numerators together and denominators together: (a/b) × (c/d) = (a×c) / (b×d).
  • Dividing: Multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction: (a/b) ÷ (c/d) = (a×d) / (b×c).
