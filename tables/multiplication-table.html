Multiplication Facts & Tips

  • Zero Property: Any number multiplied by 0 equals 0: a × 0 = 0
  • Identity Property: Any number multiplied by 1 equals itself: a × 1 = a
  • Commutative Property: Numbers can be multiplied in any order: a × b = b × a (e.g. 6 × 7 = 7 × 6 = 42).
