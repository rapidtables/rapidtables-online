How Online PDF Compression Works

PDF files often contain uncompressed high-resolution images, redundant metadata, and un-optimized vector streams. RapidTables.online analyzes every page of your PDF, downsamples embedded bitmaps to optimal web resolutions (DPI), recompresses photographic streams using efficient JPEG encoding, and removes extraneous document bloat.

Features & Compression Levels

  • Recommended: 150 DPI re-sampling with 65% quality JPEG encoding. Produces an average of 40% to 75% size reduction while keeping all typography razor sharp.
  • Extreme: 100 DPI re-sampling with 40% quality. Best when you must satisfy strict email attachment caps (e.g. 5MB or 10MB limits).
  • Low: 200 DPI re-sampling with 85% quality. Retains near-lossless clarity for high-end presentation documents.
  • 100% In-Browser Privacy: Your sensitive invoices, tax returns, and legal records are never uploaded to any remote server or stored in any database. Everything occurs in local browser RAM.
