How to Extract Specific Pages from a PDF

  1. Upload PDF: Select your file to view high-resolution visual previews of each page.
  2. Choose Pages: Click the thumbnails you want to extract or type custom page ranges like 1-3, 6, 8-10.
  3. Export: Click "Extract to 1 PDF" to combine the chosen pages into one neat document, or "Separate PDFs (ZIP)" to save each page as an individual file.
