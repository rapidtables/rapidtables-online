Supported Barcode Standards & Formats

Code 128

High-density alphanumeric barcode. Supports all 128 ASCII characters. Widely used for logistics, shipping containers, and asset tracking.

EAN-13 & UPC-A

Standard retail product barcodes scanned at supermarket point-of-sale checkouts worldwide (EAN in Europe/Asia/Latin America, UPC in USA/Canada).

Code 39 & ITF-14

Standard in industrial, automotive, aerospace, and defense applications. ITF-14 is standard for corrugated master shipping cartons.

Print & Scanner Tips

Maintain a clean white Quiet Zone (margin) of at least 10px on both sides so standard laser and camera barcode readers can detect the start and stop characters accurately.
