How to Add Page Numbers to PDF Files

  1. Upload PDF: Select your document from your computer or phone.
  2. Select Position: Click one of the 6 position buttons (e.g. Bottom Right or Bottom Center).
  3. Customize Style: Choose whether you want "Page X of Y", "X / Y", or simple numerals, and toggle whether to skip the first cover page.
  4. Download: Click "Add Page Numbers & Download" to receive your numbered PDF document immediately.

Ideal for Legal, Academic, and Business Reports

Whether formatting a thesis, dissertation, court filing, corporate proposal, or multi-chapter eBook, clear page numbering is essential. Our tool stamps uniform, high-contrast numbers across your document while preserving original layout, margins, and vector text quality.
