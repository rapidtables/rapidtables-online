Why Are Meta Tags Critical for SEO?

Meta tags provide structured data about your webpage directly to search engine crawlers (Google, Bing) and social media platforms:

  • Page Title: The single most important on-page SEO signal. Keep under 60 characters to avoid truncation in Google search results.
  • Meta Description: Compels users to click on your search snippet. Keep between 140 and 160 characters.
  • Open Graph (OG) Tags: Designed by Facebook and used universally across LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Slack, and Discord to generate rich previews.
  • Twitter Cards: Enhances tweets with large visual imagery and concise summaries.
