How to Edit and Annotate PDF Documents Online

  1. Upload PDF: Select your file to open it in the visual editor.
  2. Choose a Tool:
    • Add Text: Type your words, pick font color and size, and click anywhere to insert.
    • Draw Pen: Sketch freehand signatures, circles, checkmarks, or arrows.
    • Highlight: Drag a translucent highlighter over important sections.
    • Stamp: One-click stamp approvals like APPROVED, CONFIDENTIAL, or PAID.
  3. Export: Click "Save & Download PDF" to get your newly annotated file instantly.
