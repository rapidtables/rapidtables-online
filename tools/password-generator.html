What Makes a Password Strong?

  • Length: 16 characters or longer is recommended for modern security standards.
  • Character Variety: Mixing uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols vastly expands the search space (entropy).
  • Unpredictability: True cryptographically random character generation makes brute-force attacks infeasible.
