How to record a call:

1. Select Dual Call Mode.
2. Click Start Call Recording.
3. When prompted, select the tab with your active call (e.g., Google Meet, WhatsApp Web) and ensure "Share audio" is checked.
4. When your call finishes, click Stop to save your audio.
