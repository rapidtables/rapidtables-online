Understanding Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)

To ensure that websites and software applications are accessible to users with low vision, color blindness, or age-related visual impairments, the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) established the WCAG 2.1 standard:

  • Level AA (Minimum Legal Standard): Requires a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (18pt or 14pt bold). Most international laws (ADA, Section 508, European EN 301 549) require Level AA compliance.
  • Level AAA (Enhanced Standard): Requires a contrast ratio of at least 7:1 for normal body text and 4.5:1 for large text.
  • UI Components: Interactive form inputs, borders, and functional icons must achieve at least 3:1 contrast against their adjacent background.
