Permanent vs. Cosmetic Redaction

Many standard software programs merely draw a black rectangle on top of PDF text, leaving the underlying sensitive letters selectable and extractable by copying or inspecting. RapidTables.online burns your redaction boxes directly into the page raster stream, destroying the original text layer permanently so it can never be recovered.

What to Redact Before Sharing

  • Personal Identifiers: Social security numbers, tax IDs, passport numbers, home addresses, phone numbers.
  • Financial Details: Bank account numbers, routing codes, credit card digits, transaction balances.
  • Legal & Business Data: Client identities, proprietary formulas, confidential settlements, signature stamps.
  • 100% In-Browser Privacy: Your sensitive files are processed in local browser RAM with zero server-side transmission.
