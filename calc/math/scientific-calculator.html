About the Scientific Calculator

This online scientific calculator performs both basic arithmetic (+, -, ×, ÷) and advanced scientific mathematics directly in your web browser:

  • Trigonometry: Sine (sin), Cosine (cos), Tangent (tan) with configurable Degree (DEG) or Radian (RAD) mode.
  • Logarithmic Functions: Base-10 logarithm (log) and natural logarithm (ln).
  • Powers and Roots: Square root (√) and custom exponents (x^y).
  • Constants & Factorials: Mathematical constants Pi (π ≈ 3.14159), Euler's number (e ≈ 2.71828), and factorial (n!).
