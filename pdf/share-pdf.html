Private & Secure Document Sharing

RapidTables.online utilizes the browser's native Web Share API to hand off documents directly to your operating system's sharing sheet. This guarantees your files pass directly to your selected contact or application without passing through intermediate third-party hosting servers.

Supported Share Targets

  • Messaging Apps: WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Slack, and Microsoft Teams.
  • Email Clients: Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, and Thunderbird.
