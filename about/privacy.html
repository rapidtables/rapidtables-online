Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Strict Zero-Log Client Architecture

Privacy Commitment Summary: RapidTables.online does not require an account, does not harvest personal identifying information (PII), and performs file operations (PDF merging, text editing, QR generation) directly in your browser. Your files never get uploaded to or stored on our servers.

1. Zero-Log Client-Side Architecture

Unlike conventional online services that require you to upload documents to external servers for processing, RapidTables.online is engineered with a modern, browser-native architecture.

Tools such as our PDF Suite (Merge, Split, Watermark, Sign), Online Notepad, Image Compressor, and QR / Barcode Generator execute 100% locally on your machine via JavaScript, Canvas, and WebAssembly APIs. Your files are processed entirely in your computer's temporary memory (RAM) and are discarded as soon as you close the browser tab.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect names, home addresses, or phone numbers.
  • We do not retain copies of uploaded PDF files, images, or source code.
  • We do not record financial inputs entered into calculators (such as loan amounts, salary numbers, or mortgage figures).
  • We do not track passwords generated using our Password Generator.

3. Local Storage Policy (Client-Side Only)

We use standard browser localStorage solely to remember your immediate user interface preferences across sessions:

  • Theme Preference: Storing whether you prefer Light Mode or Dark Mode (rapidtables_theme).
  • Online Notepad Drafts: Notes you write in the Notepad tool are saved strictly inside your browser's private local database so your work is preserved across refreshes. You can clear this anytime with one click.

4. Hosting & Security Standards

Our web delivery servers are hosted in secure modern facilities equipped with:

  • TLS 1.3 Encryption: All communications between your browser and our servers are encrypted via modern SSL/TLS certificates with strict HTTPS enforcement.
  • HSTS & Security Headers: X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN, and X-XSS-Protection headers are active to defend against clickjacking and cross-site scripting attacks.
  • Standard Server Telemetry: Like all standard web servers, anonymous network connection logs (IP address, user-agent, timestamp, requested URI) are temporarily processed for rate limiting, DDoS defense, and infrastructure health monitoring. These logs contain no personal document data.

5. Content Delivery Networks (CDNs)

To ensure instantaneous load speeds globally, select static open-source libraries (such as pdf-lib, JsBarcode) are served through trusted CDNs (Cloudflare, jsDelivr, CDNJS) utilizing Subresource Integrity (SRI) to guarantee that script files cannot be tampered with.

6. GDPR (EU) & CCPA (California) Compliance

Because RapidTables.online does not maintain user databases, sell personal information, or monetize user identities, we fully adhere to the core principles of the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

You have the absolute right to visit and use all tools anonymously without consent barriers or trackers.

7. Contacting Us Regarding Privacy

If you have questions about our data protection standards or wish to submit feedback, please visit our Contact & Feedback page.
