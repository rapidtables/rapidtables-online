Standard Tipping Guidelines

Tipping etiquette varies around the world, especially in North American dining culture:

  • 15%: Standard baseline for polite, satisfactory table service.
  • 18%: Standard for good restaurant service.
  • 20%: Excellent, attentive service.
  • 25%+: Outstanding hospitality, exceptional dining experiences, or large group tables.
  • Food Delivery & Taxi: Commonly 10% to 15%, or a minimum of $3 to $5.
