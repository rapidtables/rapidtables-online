How to Add a Watermark to a PDF File

  1. Upload PDF: Choose your document from your computer or mobile device.
  2. Customize Watermark: Type your custom watermark text or choose standard presets such as CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, or SAMPLE.
  3. Adjust Appearance: Fine-tune rotation angle (e.g. 45° diagonal), opacity slider (so underlying text remains readable), and color.
  4. Download: Click "Apply Watermark & Download PDF". The document is stamped instantly on your machine.

Why Watermark Your Documents?

Watermarking protects confidential contracts, intellectual property, unreleased manuscripts, academic research, and draft proposals from unauthorized distribution or plagiarism. By imprinting transparent security markings across each page, recipients are immediately reminded of the document's legal status.
