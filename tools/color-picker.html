Color Models Explained

  • HEX (#RRGGBB): Hexadecimal representation of Red, Green, and Blue intensity from 00 to FF (0 to 255).
  • RGB (Red, Green, Blue): Additive color model where colors are created by combining primary colors of light.
  • HSL (Hue, Saturation, Lightness): Cylindrical-coordinate representation that aligns closer to human perception of color.
