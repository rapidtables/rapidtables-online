Text Case Converter

Quickly change the letter capitalization of any text into standard writing styles and programming formats.

Supported Text Transformations

  • UPPERCASE: All characters converted to capital letters.
  • lowercase: All characters converted to small letters.
  • Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of each word.
  • Sentence case: Capitalizes the first letter of each sentence.
  • camelCase: Concatenates words with capitalization of subsequent words (e.g. myVariableName).
  • snake_case: Separates lowercased words with underscores (e.g. my_variable_name).
  • kebab-case: Separates words with hyphens for URLs (e.g. my-page-title).
