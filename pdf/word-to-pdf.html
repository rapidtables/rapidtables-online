How Word to PDF Conversion Works

Our client-side engine parses the internal XML markup of your .docx document using Mammoth, restoring typography, headings, tables, bullet lists, and paragraphs. It then compiles the document into vector PDF format using high-definition rendering.

Why Convert DOCX to PDF?

  • Universal Layout: Word documents often render differently across versions of MS Office. PDFs look 100% identical on phones, Mac, Windows, and Linux.
  • Print & Submit Ready: Resumes, contracts, and assignments are universally accepted in PDF format.
  • 100% Private: Your files never travel across the internet. Instant conversion in local browser memory.
