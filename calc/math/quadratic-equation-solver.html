Quadratic Formula

Roots of the quadratic equation ax² + bx + c = 0 are given by:

x = [ -b ± √(b² - 4ac) ] / (2a)

The term Δ = b² - 4ac is the discriminant:

  • Δ > 0: Two distinct real roots.
  • Δ = 0: Exactly one real root (double root).
  • Δ < 0: Two conjugate complex roots.
