Energy Consumption Formula

The energy consumed by an electrical appliance per day in kilowatt-hours (kWh):

Energy (kWh/day) = Power (Watts) × Time (Hours/day) / 1000
Cost ($/day) = Energy (kWh/day) × Price ($/kWh)
