Mifflin-St Jeor BMR Equation

The Mifflin-St Jeor formula calculates Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR):

Men: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) - (5 × age) + 5
Women: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) - (5 × age) - 161

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) is then calculated by multiplying BMR with your physical activity multiplier.
