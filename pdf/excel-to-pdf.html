How Excel to PDF Conversion Works

Our in-browser parser loads your workbook via SheetJS, extracts the active sheet's rows, headers, and cell values, and formats them into a clean, paginated PDF document. Landscape orientation is enabled by default to preserve wide spreadsheet columns without truncation.

Key Benefits

  • Confidential Accounting: Financial figures, bank audits, and company payrolls never touch remote cloud servers.
  • Multi-Sheet Navigation: Seamlessly toggle between tabs to export specific sheets.
  • Clean Grid Styling: Formats cell borders and alternating zebra rows for optimal readability on printouts and screens.
