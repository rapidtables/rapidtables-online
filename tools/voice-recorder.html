How to Record Voice and Sound Online

  1. Click Microphone: Press the red microphone button to begin. Your browser will ask for microphone permission.
  2. Speak Clearly: Watch the real-time visual waveform respond to your voice volume and frequency.
  3. Pause or Finish: Pause if you need a break, or click "Finish & Save" when done.
  4. Listen & Download: Preview your recording with the built-in media player and download the audio file directly to your device.

100% Private & Secure

Unlike cloud voice recorders that transmit your private conversations to remote third-party databases, our tool records entirely inside your browser memory using HTML5 MediaRecorder. Your audio never touches any external server.
