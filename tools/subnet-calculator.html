What is an IPv4 Subnet Calculator?

Subnetting is the practice of dividing a physical network into smaller, logical sub-networks (subnets). Network engineers and system administrators calculate subnets to optimize IP allocation, reduce broadcast traffic, and enforce firewall security boundaries across LANs and cloud VPCs (such as AWS and Google Cloud).

Common CIDR Prefix Reference:

  • /24 (255.255.255.0): 256 Total IPs, 254 Usable Hosts (Most common home and office network).
  • /28 (255.255.255.240): 16 Total IPs, 14 Usable Hosts.
  • /30 (255.255.255.252): 4 Total IPs, 2 Usable Hosts (Ideal for point-to-point router links).
  • /16 (255.255.0.0): 65,536 Total IPs, 65,534 Usable Hosts.
